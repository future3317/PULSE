## 4. Critical finding: `manuscript_numbers.json` portfolio primary-best mismatch

`results/phase7c/manuscript_numbers.json` records:

```json
"primary_best_strategy": "balanced_union",
"primary_best_worst_source_recall": 0.5789473684210527,
"primary_best_paired_diff_recall": 0.0141129032258064
```

However, the authoritative CSV `results/phase7c/portfolio_benchmark.csv` for **P0 / F1_Frobenius / full_panel / budget_factor=1.0** shows:

| Strategy | Worst-source recall | Paired diff recall | 95% CI low | 95% CI high |
|----------|---------------------|--------------------|------------|-------------|
| `balanced_union` | **0.5263157894736842** | **0.012096774193548387** | **-0.012096774193548387** | **0.036290322580645164** |
| `average_percentile` | 0.45614035087719296 | 0.014112903225806451 | -0.012147177419354836 | 0.04233870967741935 |

The v0.6 LaTeX macros are correct:

```latex
\newcommand{\PrimaryBestStrategy}{balanced\_union}
\newcommand{\PrimaryBestWorstSourceRecall}{0.526}
\newcommand{\PrimaryBestPairedDiffRecall}{+0.012 (95\% CI [-0.012, 0.036])}
```

### Diagnosis

`manuscript_numbers.json` contains a generation bug:

- `primary_best_strategy` is labelled `balanced_union`, but
- `primary_best_worst_source_recall` equals the `average_percentile` recall at **budget_factor=2.0** (0.5789...), and
- `primary_best_paired_diff_recall` equals the `average_percentile` paired difference at **budget_factor=1.0** (0.0141...).

The CSV artifact and the v0.6 manuscript are internally consistent and should be treated as authoritative. The manifest hash correctly binds the buggy JSON file, so the issue is in the Phase 7C JSON generation step, not in the manifest itself.

### Impact

- The v0.6 manuscript is **not** affected; its numbers are correct.
- Any v0.7 manuscript or corrected `manuscript_numbers.json` must use the CSV-derived values:
  - `primary_best_worst_source_recall = 0.526`
  - `primary_best_paired_diff_recall = +0.012 (95% CI [-0.012, 0.036])`
- The Phase 8A portfolio claim must be frozen at the equal-budget `balanced_union` values.
