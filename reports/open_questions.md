# CrossPiezo / PULSE Open Questions

> Generated by the Phase 0-4 audit pipeline.  These questions must be resolved
> before any Full-Go PULSE development.

## Data and provenance

1. The T2C-Flow overlap file provides 1,266 formula-equal JARVIS–MP pairs, but
   its own note warns that tensor components are **not directly comparable**
   because source structures / IEEE frames and calculation revisions may differ.
   How should the strict-pair tensor discrepancy be validated against an
   independent cross-reference?
2. MP and JARVIS piezoelectric tensors both claim units of `C/m^2`, but are the
   underlying source conventions (stress vs. strain, IEEE frame, sign) fully
   equivalent?  A convention audit from the original downloads is still missing.
3. PiezoJet strict-factor records are keyed by `sha256(jid)[:16]`.  How many of
   the 538 Tier 1 pairs also have complete Born-charge / internal-strain /
   force-constant payloads, and is that subset large enough for the soft-mode
   mechanism test?

## Methodology

4. The structure-match rotation aligns CIF lattices, but the piezoelectric
   Cartesian tensors may be reported in source-specific standard orientations.
   Does rotating the MP tensor into the JARVIS CIF frame produce a physically
   consistent comparison, or does it introduce artificial disagreement?
5. Point-group projection using the CIF-setting space group produced non-trivial
   symmetry residuals for many JARVIS records.  Does this reflect genuine
   source-data convention differences, or an orientation mismatch between the
   CIF and the reported tensor frame?
6. The normalized discrepancy median is ~1.6.  Is this magnitude dominated by
   (a) real protocol differences, (b) remaining convention/orientation errors,
   or (c) near-zero tensors?  A sensitivity analysis excluding small-norm and
   high-residual records is needed.

## Model-error benchmark

7. The Phase 0-4 audit did not collect or reproduce audited in-source model
   errors.  Without these, PMR cannot be computed and H1 cannot be tested.
   What is the lightest reproducible baseline that can provide a comparable
   in-source error scale?

## Decision

8. With 538 Tier 1 pairs and strongly unstable rankings (top-50 Jaccard 0.08,
   Kendall tau 0.26), the project meets a **Narrow Go** for a benchmark/audit
   paper.  Does it meet the stricter **Full Go** criteria for PULSE development?
   The current answer is **no**, pending PMR and soft-mode evidence.
