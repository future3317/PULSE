# CrossPiezo claim boundary

This document defines what the current two-source benchmark can and cannot
support. It is a live policy document and intentionally has no manual date.

## Supported claims

- Cross-source disagreement is a source-conditional measurement, not
  experimental truth.
- Global rank correlation and elite-tail overlap are different properties of a
  screening workflow.
- MP and JARVIS can be compared on a frozen, structure-matched panel after
  explicit convention handling.
- A simple two-source portfolio can be presented as a risk-management
  illustration under a fixed budget.

## Claims requiring independent adjudication

Do not claim any of the following without an independent third protocol or
experiment:

- true tensor, ground-truth consensus, or physically exact ranking;
- experimental validation or physical correctness of either source;
- a protocol uncertainty floor or a general limit on AI screening;
- robust discovery, calibration, or generalization beyond the evaluated panel.

Do not call an average of MP and JARVIS a physical tensor. Do not call a
two-source portfolio a validated method comparison.

## Required qualifiers

- State the evaluated sources, panel, metric, quantile range, and uncertainty
  interval for exact claims.
- Treat undocumented source workflow details as unknown rather than assuming
  identical functionals, pseudopotentials, DFPT settings, or frames.
- Keep conventional property controls as workflow-sensitivity evidence; they do
  not identify the physical cause of piezoelectric disagreement.
