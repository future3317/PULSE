# CrossPiezo Invariant Manuscript: Venue Positioning

## Candidate venues

### 1. Scientific Data
- **Contribution fit**: High. The paper is primarily a curated, versioned benchmark with reproducibility artifacts and hash-bound result tables.
- **Validation**: Strong---independent statistics verification, frozen panels, and result manifest meet Scientific Data's emphasis on reproducibility.
- **Data release**: Required; we provide CrossPiezo-Invariant-v1 cards and reconstruction instructions.
- **Method novelty**: Low direct method novelty; this is a benchmark paper, not a new model.
- **Likely reviewer objections**: Scope may be seen as narrow if only two databases; need to emphasize the invariant-metric and cross-database-concordance angle.

### 2. Digital Discovery (RSC)
- **Contribution fit**: Good. Focus on data-driven discovery and benchmarking.
- **Validation**: Strong; reproducibility and independent verification align with journal standards.
- **Data release**: Expected; our cards and manifest satisfy this.
- **Method novelty**: Moderate; coordinate-invariant metrics and exact null statistics are methodologically interesting but not the main claim.
- **Likely reviewer objections**: May ask for broader database coverage or a third protocol; version-shift feasibility should be framed as future work.

### 3. npj Computational Materials
- **Contribution fit**: Moderate. The journal emphasizes materials insights and methodological advances; our insight is a database-definition caution rather than a new physical mechanism.
- **Validation**: Strong statistics and robustness checks help.
- **Data release**: Expected.
- **Method novelty**: Low; we do not train a new model.
- **Likely reviewer objections**: May question whether weak concordance is surprising or actionable without a third protocol; the claim must be carefully bounded.

### 4. Communications Materials
- **Contribution fit**: Moderate. Broad scope, but preference for physically motivated advances.
- **Validation**: Good.
- **Data release**: Expected.
- **Method novelty**: Low.
- **Likely reviewer objections**: Similar to npj Computational Materials; need to stress the benchmark and screening-implications framing.

## Recommendation

**Primary target: Scientific Data.** The manuscript's core contribution is a reproducible, versioned benchmark with explicit metric definitions and independent statistical audit---a natural fit for a data-journal audience. The claim is deliberately bounded (weak cross-database concordance, no physical truth), which aligns with Scientific Data's tolerance for carefully scoped datasets.

**Fallback: Digital Discovery** if reviewers at Scientific Data request more methodological novelty.

## No journal promises
No venue guarantees acceptance. The choice should be revisited after a co-author or advisor review of the final manuscript.
